# Attribution

- **CSI-BFI-HAR dataset** — Khandaker Foysal Haque, *CSI-BFI-HAR: Wi-Fi Datasets for Human Activity Recognition*, IEEE DataPort, 2025. DOI: [10.21227/nbp8-7r47](https://doi.org/10.21227/nbp8-7r47). [Dataset files and documentation](https://huggingface.co/datasets/foysalhaque/CSI-BFI-HAR-Dataset).
- **BeamSense** — Khandaker Foysal Haque, Milin Zhang, Francesca Meneghello, and Francesco Restuccia, *BeamSense: Rethinking Wireless Sensing with MU-MIMO Wi-Fi Beamforming Feedback*, Computer Networks 258, 111020, 2025. DOI: [10.1016/j.comnet.2024.111020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2024.111020). The sensing trainer follows the public CNN layer sequence. The generator includes a PyTorch equivalent for gradients through a frozen Keras teacher; this is not a new sensing architecture.
- **BFI decoding** — `tfdiff/bfa_har.py` documents the relationship to `pcap_to_bfa.m` for HAR-1 decoding. Prior extraction project: [CSI-BFI-HAR](https://github.com/kfoysalhaque/CSI-BFI-HAR).
